Make it, see it, sign off on it.

Chatterbox is built around what your agents make, not the commands they ran to make it.

Make

Ask for a thing. Get the thing.

When an agent writes a web page or an SVG, it renders live in the reply. Images land as images, ready to copy.

Open

Open it wherever suits you.

Click a page to open it in a sidebar beside the chat, full screen, in its own window or in your browser.

Collect

Every image, in one place.

The Image Library gathers everything a chat or Studio has made, with names and dates, so version three is easy to find.

Review

See what needs you.

Home shows every chat as a card with its latest artwork. Filter to Needs you and work through the queue.
