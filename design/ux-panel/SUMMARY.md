## Agreed strengths

- The Desk puts what needs you first, with actions on the card (all four).
- The Start There step: place, existing sessions, what will happen (Opus, Fable, Luna).
- Agent work is readable: tool rows, plan, failures, elapsed time (all four).

## Agreed problems (judges naming it)

1. Approvals differ by surface; the iPhone Desk hides the command; Approve vs Allow; no "for this
   session" on the Desk; Mac buttons all the same grey (Opus, Fable, Sol).
2. Contrast: grey secondary text, light-mode yellow, faint needs-you border (all four).
3. Status told by color alone, and color also means which agent (Opus, Fable, Sol).
4. Mac has two navigation models and unlabeled icon rails (Opus, Fable, Sol; Luna: Desk differs by platform).
5. Offline: cached "3 working" still spins; no Retry or last-updated (Opus, Sol, Luna).
6. Names drift: model names, X-High vs Extra High, permission names, duplicate labels (Opus, Fable, Luna).
7. Phone Desk too sparse to triage; the tab bar covers content (Opus, Sol, Luna).
8. Silence too easy by full swipe, no undo, nothing shows hidden items (Sol, Luna).
9. Mac selection and needs-you look alike (Opus, Fable).
10. Single judges: badge counts overstate (Fable); answer short questions on the Desk (Fable);
    Bypass permission is the loudest tile in New Chat (Opus); Codex pane chip reads "Claude ·
    gpt-6.1-sol" (Luna).
